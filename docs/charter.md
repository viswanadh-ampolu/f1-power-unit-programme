# Programme Charter — Project Apex (2026 Power Unit)

## 1. Vision
Deliver a **race-winning, reliable, cost-cap-compliant** F1 power unit for the 2026
regulations — on the grid for round 1, inside the permitted element allocation.

## 2. The three hard constraints
Every decision is weighed against these. They do not move.

1. **FIA homologation deadline** — the PU specification must be submitted and approved
   by the regulatory deadline. After homologation the design is locked for the season.
2. **Power Unit Cost Cap** — total PU spend must stay under the regulatory financial cap.
3. **Element allocation** — each driver may use only a limited number of each PU element
   across the season before grid penalties apply; reliability must support a full season.

## 3. Objectives & success measures
| Objective | Measure | Baseline (concept) | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Performance | Peak power vs internal target | 94% | ≥ 100% |
| Reliability | Validated race distance per element | 3,800 km | ≥ 7,000 km |
| Sustainable fuel | Compliant 100% sustainable fuel integrated | Lab only | Race-ready |
| Cost | PU programme spend vs cost cap | — | ≤ 98% of cap |
| Deployment | Homologation approved on first submission | — | Yes |

## 4. Scope
**In scope:** ICE (combustion + sustainable fuel), ERS/battery & electrical deployment,
reliability & dyno validation, cost-cap tracking, FIA homologation, trackside integration
and in-season support.
**Out of scope:** chassis/aero, gearbox (interface dependency only), customer-team supply
(tracked separately).

## 5. Workstreams
1. **ICE & Combustion** — engine architecture, sustainable fuel calibration.
2. **ERS & Battery** — energy recovery/deployment, battery, control electronics.
3. **Reliability & Dyno** — validation programme, mileage accumulation, failure analysis.
4. **Cost Cap & FIA Compliance** — financial tracking, homologation dossier, audit readiness.
5. **Trackside Integration** — install, telemetry, in-season deployment & support.

## 6. Phases
- **Phase 1 — Concept & architecture (M1–M5):** design freeze candidates, rig testing, cost model.
- **Phase 2 — Validation & homologation (M6–M12):** dyno mileage, reliability sign-off, FIA submission.
- **Phase 3 — Deployment & season (M13–M18):** pre-season testing, race rollout, in-season support.

## 7. Budget
Managed against the **Power Unit Cost Cap**. Internal split (indicative): 40% ICE,
28% ERS/battery, 18% reliability/dyno, 8% compliance, 6% contingency.

## 8. Governance (summary)
Monthly **PU Steering Board**, weekly **dyno & reliability review** (resource + failures),
daily **trackside sync** in-season. One hard **homologation gate** at end of Phase 2.
Full model in [governance.md](governance.md).

## 10. Top-level risks (full log in [raid-log.md](raid-log.md))
- Reliability target not met before homologation lock.
- Cost-cap pressure forces scope/spec trade-offs.
- Dyno capacity is the shared bottleneck across all workstreams.
